## Implementation status — September 8, 2026

Added 59 planner entries from 46 candidate rows: 42 entries with approximate host-area map locations and 17 school-administered or host-to-confirm entries. Some rows expand into multiple seasons, grade contests, or in-person finals. Candidate numbers are retained in the catalog for auditing.

- Added rows 1–38, 40–42, 44, and 47–50.
- Excluded online/remote-only rows 39 (USAMTS), 43 (Caribou), 45 (HiMCM), and 46 (MidMCM).
- Hybrid contests contribute their in-person division only. MathCON contributes its Chicago final, with online qualification explained.
- School-based CEMC, Noetic, and Math League programs have no fabricated map location. Hawaii Math Bowl and TMSCA remain host-to-confirm until a specific edition's venue is verified.
- Old editions do not acquire invented future dates or deadlines. Tentative dates are identified and excluded from calendar export.
- The three originally uncertain leads were resolved using the Yale Math Competitions homepage, Interlake organizer search results, and Rockdale's organizer site plus the Georgia Council of Teachers of Mathematics calendar.
- Existing online entries were preserved; no new online-only entries were added.

Each catalog entry contains organizer sources, a check date, and deadline notes where relevant. Approximate host-area coordinates use the bundled GeoNames US postal dataset, with precision displayed in the list. A previous host is not a promise about the next venue.
