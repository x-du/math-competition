The live list is on the [competition map and calendar](../planner.html#calendar): dates, a US map, mile filters, and a schedule you can save in this browser. This page is a short snapshot of widely followed contests. **Listings are for discovery, not endorsement.** Confirm dates, eligibility, and registration with each organizer. Unannounced editions are left as TBD; last year’s date is not reused.
